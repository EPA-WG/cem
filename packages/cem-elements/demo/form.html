Form data and validation

A form with slice captures its named controls under datadom.formData.<slice> as controls emit events and when the form submits. The same data is mirrored under the form slice and is the source of truth for rendering and validation.

The custom-validity expression can return a boolean or a message string. A false boolean prevents submission; a string makes the form or control invalid and supplies its validation message. Native and custom messages are available through datadom.validationState.

See the browser APIs behind these examples in FormData and Constraint validation.
